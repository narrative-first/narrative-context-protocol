# Repository Publication Policy

This repository publishes the open technical material needed to implement, exchange, inspect, and structurally validate Narrative Context Protocol documents.

## Public protocol material

The public NCP package includes:

- NCP Core schemas and specifications;
- separately versioned profile, extension, serialization, and binding contracts;
- the Dramatica Storyform Profile's transport structure, terminology, definitions, direct mappings, and preservation guidance;
- synthetic examples and negative fixtures;
- structural validation tools;
- compatibility and migration guidance; and
- namespace, contribution, conformance, versioning, and licensing policies.

Earlier combined schemas remain available as frozen compatibility artifacts. Their presence does not make them part of the NCP 3 layered normative path.

## Publication boundaries

Public NCP material may explain what a transported value means and how to preserve it without implementing the private intelligence that creates or evaluates that value.

The repository does not publish:

- Dramatica valid-combination or Storyform-generation logic;
- private structural matrices, resolution rules, or diagnostic algorithms;
- automated completion, semantic derivation, or semantic-validation technology;
- certification technology, private corpora, or advanced coaching systems;
- confidential, personal, or restricted production data; or
- third-party material without permission to publish it.

These boundaries keep the interchange contract implementable while preserving the distinction between open structural validation and separately licensed narrative-system intelligence.

## Maintained namespaces

NCP Core and maintained namespaces follow the change, compatibility, and migration requirements in [Namespace Governance](namespaces.md), [Conformance](conformance.md), and [Versioning](versioning.md). Third-party concepts use their own declared namespaces rather than redefining Core or the official Dramatica Storyform Profile.
